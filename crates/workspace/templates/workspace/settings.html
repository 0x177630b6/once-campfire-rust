Each department is a Fizzy tag on the cards of {{ board_name }}. Rooms linked to a department show its open cards in a side panel, and new cards created there get its tag.

What Hermes may do alone

When Hermes proposes an action through Campfire: Alone runs it at once (it’s in the Hermes tab and can be undone for 24 hours), Ask first posts it as a draft that someone confirms (per the rule above; 24 hours to answer), Never refuses it. A live voice report its reporter confirmed is filed at once unless creating cards is set to Never.

This only hides cards in Campfire (Home, the board, room panels, card chips and sheets, mentions, the Hermes tab, alerts, the handover). Anyone with a Fizzy login sees the whole board in Fizzy itself. A room’s members are whoever was added to it, so turn on Campfire’s “Only administrators can create rooms” (Account settings): otherwise anyone can create a room, and whoever an administrator links it to a department grants its members that department’s cards. A restricted department can’t be linked to an open room (one everyone is in): saving refuses it.

Handover

Duty managers prepare it from Home: a summary built from the board, which they edit and post under their own name. Nothing is written to Fizzy.
